What we ask for

Our contact form asks for:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or email
  • Your message (what you need)

How we use it

We only use this information to reply to you and prepare your quote. The form is delivered to our email inbox through Web3Forms, the service that sends the form to us.

We don't sell or share it

We never sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Deleting your information

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by writing to blueprintappsweb@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking cookies or ads. It only remembers, in your own browser, the language you choose.
