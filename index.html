    Frequently asked questions

    What people ask us most

    Short, clear answers. If your question isn't here, send us a message.

    How much does a website cost in Las Cruces?

    Our websites start at $275 (Express, a single page). Basic starts at $500, Complete at $950 and a custom app at $1,200. Quotes are free, and the final price is in writing before we start.

    How long does it take?

    An Express page is ready in a few days. Larger sites and apps depend on how many pages and features they need; the proposal we send you includes the dates.

    Do I need a domain (.com)?

    Not to get started. Every project includes help connecting your domain, and if you don't have one yet, we'll help you choose and register it.

    How does payment work?

    A 50% deposit when you approve the proposal and the remaining 50% when your site goes live. Prices are in US dollars and don't include New Mexico GRT.

    Can I make changes myself later?

    Yes. When we launch, we show you how to use your site. If you'd rather have us update text and photos, the maintenance plan is $35 a month and includes WhatsApp support.

    Do you build websites in Spanish and English?

    Yes. We work with you in the language you prefer and can build your site in English, Spanish or both. The Complete plan already includes both languages.
